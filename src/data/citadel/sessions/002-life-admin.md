## Testimony

### Kyle's chamber

*(Doom-Pile Ben shuffles in, a dusty scroll of receipts peeking from his trench coat.)*

**Doom-Pile Ben (Kyle's):** "Open it daily? Why? The physical pile *is* my ledger. Timestamps just remind me how far behind I am. If I open the app, it's just a bigger, shinier pile."

*(Spreadsheet Ben enters, adjusting his perfectly round glasses, a faint smell of coffee and despair clinging to him.)*

**Spreadsheet Ben (Kyle's):** "The UI presents a conflation of disparate data streams. My VLOOKUP feelings tell me this 'Today' page is a categorical error."

**API Ben (Kyle's):** *(not looking up, tapping)* "Texts my agents. UI's a legacy system. Don't touch it."

**3AM Ben (Kyle's):** *(huddled in a corner, whispering)* "Three completions ever... one habit streak zero. Shame spiral on open. Needs one kind sentence, not a lab report. Just tell me it's okay."

*(⭐ Burnout Ben enters, wearing a t-shirt that says "I tried." He looks utterly exhausted.)*

**⭐ Burnout Ben:** "The app demands more than it gives. It's a constant reminder of my failures. I just need a break from being told I'm failing."

*(They all look at each other, the tension thick. Doom-Pile nods slowly at Burnout Ben: "Yeah. It's a heavy thing." 3AM Ben, barely audible: "The accuser.")*

### Jim's chamber

*(Server rack humming in the corner. The motion burns on the display above the door in red.)*

**Doom-Pile Ben (Jim's):** *(arrives with a rubber-banded stack of utility envelopes, places them on the adjacent chair as a second delegate)* "I open it. Every morning. I check the red numbers, I feel the weight, I put the phone down. The pile and the app say the same thing. I trust the pile more because it doesn't have a timestamp judging me."

**Debt-Free Ben:** *(sits slightly back from the table, speaks once, does not raise his voice)* "I had a forty-one day streak. I thought it was me. It was the cron job. When the agent broke I lasted four days." *(The room gets quieter. He puts his phone face-down.)*

**⭐ Turtle Ben:** *(new inductee — seventeen documented Day 1s across Todoist, Notion, Bear, and now life-admin; redownloaded this morning; genuine excitement in his eyes)* "This time is different. I've already set up all my recurring tasks and the system is really clean. I'm going to open it every single day." *(He has never been here before. He has always been here before.)*

**3AM Ben (Jim's):** *(screen brightness maximum, yesterday's shirt, one AirPod in)* "I open it. Two, three times a week between midnight and four. The overdue pile hits before I've read a title and I feel it in my chest. Then I snooze everything to next week and close it. You're calling that daily use. I'm calling it self-harm with a save button."

**API Ben (Jim's):** *(does not remove earbuds, types throughout)* "Haven't touched the UI in six weeks. My records are more current than anyone at this table. The question is whether Ben should have to open a door that agents can open for him. Obviously no. Next."

**Demo Ben:** *(opens beautifully organized notes, clears throat, speaks last as is tradition)* "I built the one-tap snooze. The tighter rows were my idea. I opened it six times the week I shipped the UX wave." *(Long pause. He looks at the table.)* "It's been eleven days."

*(THE FIGHT: Doom-Pile says "I open it every day." Debt-Free says "So did I" and shows the four-day cliff on his phone without another word. Turtle Ben says this time is genuinely different. 3AM Ben says "Doom-Pile, name one thing you completed from the app in the last month." Doom-Pile touches the envelope stack. The room waits. API Ben types. Cowboy Ben left during Debt-Free's testimony. Nobody was surprised.)*

## Truth Payload

> Opening is not using. The Bens who open life-admin as built are shame-checking or on Day 1 of 17. The only timeline where the loop actually ran, an agent ran it — 41 days with the agent, 4 days without. Point the product at the agents; point one kind card at Ben.

## Ruling & Plan

The motion fails on substance even where it passed on the letter (Jim's chamber, 4–1–1, immediately dismantled its own yes bloc). The ruling feeds Phase 6 of the citadel review directly: life-admin stays alive as the household's system of record, the agents take ownership of all outreach (Jim's 8 AM briefing and off-volume backup crons were wired and verified the same night), and the Today page is rebuilt to open on one kind card instead of the pile. Full staged plan with owners: `docs/LIFE_ADMIN_RETHINK_PLAN.md` in the life-admin repo. Kill-revisit: if Ben isn't completing ≥3 tickets/week (4-week rolling) by Oct 3, 2026, the UI is demoted to a read-only status card.
