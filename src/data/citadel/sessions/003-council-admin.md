## Truth Payload

> The canon does not need a house; it needs an audience. Every Ben was born in conversation, and the flat files they live in already work — the only broken thing is that two of the three agents cannot write to them, and the only unbuilt thing is the publish button. Give the agents a repo, not an app.

## Ruling & Plan

The motion fails 1–13–2, and the lone yes came from the Ben for whom it is eternally Day One. The ruling kills council-admin as specified (schema, CRUD UI, Docker, port — the port was taken anyway) and redirects the effort into three stages: the canon moves to a private git repo hosted on Jim's box so all three agents can write; the already-built batkins.dev section gets published under the Council name (metric: live by Sept 15, 2026, or no new admin-app development for 12 months); the Ben Generator ships as a skill, not a service, with the debut rule intact — generator-born Bens stay pending until a session seats them. Full staged plan with owners: `COUNCIL_ADMIN_PLAN.md` in `C:\Users\benja\Lab\Council of Bens\`.
